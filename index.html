Put this on your home screen
One-time setup, about 20 seconds. These are steps to follow, not buttons.
  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's bar at the bottom of your screen (on iPad, top right). Don't see it? Tap ••• first.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top right corner.
  4. Close Safari. From now on, just tap the green Sunday Music icon.
